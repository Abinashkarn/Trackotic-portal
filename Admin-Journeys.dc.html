Journey templates

A template is expectation, not enforcement. Binds are never blocked by it; it only decides what counts as an exception. The chain itself is dynamic — a passport may travel 2 legs or 8; the patient’s PDF lists whatever legs actually happened, each as Storage or Transit.

Counts as an exception
  • A leg open longer than its expected maximum
  • A bind to a device model the template doesn't expect at that position
  • A leg closed by /handover with no next device, before the last leg
  • Same passport re-bound by another tenant with no handover scan — allowed (the new bind releases the old one) but flagged here
Never does
  • Refuse a bind — any tenant may bind to its own device
  • Show tenant names, TRK IDs or locations on the patient’s PDF
  • Limit how many legs a passport can have — binds add legs, the template just names them
  • Re-grade a leg that is already closed
