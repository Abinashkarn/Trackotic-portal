Loading
Skeleton rows in the table's own shape (grey bars, 3–5 rows). Never a spinner over a blank page. Numbers on stat cards show "—" until loaded, never 0.
Error
Inline card, red dot, plain sentence, "Retry". Keep whatever was already on screen. Request id shown small for support.
Permission
Say who can do it, not just that you can't. On bank pods: never confirm or deny that a passport exists to an unauthorised IP — same 404 page as an invalid code.
