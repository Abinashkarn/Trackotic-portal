Temperature profiles · product-wise, dynamic

A tenant gets a default profile at creation; a pharmacy-chain or lab tenant picks the profile per product at bind (a −20 vaccine and a 2–8 insulin can travel in different Pro boxes on the same day). Grace = minutes out of range before an excursion counts. The public report prints the range that applied.
