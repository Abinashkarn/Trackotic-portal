Same card as Van Health in the Axis build. A HUB shows Placed at; a Pro shows Running, with from and to; a Lite shows Out at, with the place and days out. Cards with an alert come first. The alert is also written on the card, so colour is never the only sign. Other roles see only the cards of their own Site ID.
