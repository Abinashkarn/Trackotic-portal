How it works

Our portal logins stay as they are. When this is on, every event is also pushed to the lab system, keyed by passport QR and device ID. The lab system can also ask Track0tic for the temperature record of any passport QR.

This option is switched on for the lab by Track0tic. It can be used with either portal type.
