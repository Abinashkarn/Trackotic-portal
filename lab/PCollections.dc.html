Two ways a sample is collected. At home: the collector carries a Pro box and brings it to the lab. Walk-in: the patient comes to the lab or a centre, and the tube goes straight from the collection room into the fridge, with no box and no trip.

A pickup is a pickup, whether from a hospital or a collection centre. Daily pickups are pre-defined in the Master and appear on their own. Home visits and one-off pickups are created here, or by the phone when the collector starts. A work order follows the same five steps as the Axis and Apollo builds: create, assign, start, complete, close.
