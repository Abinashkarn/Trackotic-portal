Sample data

1. What kind of trip

A walk-in at the lab needs no work order: the tube is scanned straight into the fridge.

Lab to lab: tubes already in this lab go on to another lab for the next test. To our own other lab: a Pro box with our staff. To an outside lab: a Lite box by courier. The tube keeps its label and its chain grows by one leg.

2. From and to

One temperature type per device. A different temperature type is recorded on a different device.

3. Who carries it

If one box is not enough, a second Pro is scanned onto the same work order.

4. Gate pass

Every work order has a gate pass / run sheet: printed, or shared by link or WhatsApp. The link stops working when the work order is completed or cancelled.
