A tube sits on one device at a time: a Pro box while travelling, a HUB while stored. Binding it to the next device releases it from the last one. The label is never closed, the chain only grows. When a tube goes from one lab to another lab for the next test, that is one more leg on the same label: the sending lab scans it out of its fridge into a box, the other lab scans it at its bench and decides. Each leg has its own decision, and the report shows all legs.
