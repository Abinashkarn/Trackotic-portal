Journey templates · one set per portal type

A template says which legs a passport may have and what each leg needs (scan in, scan out, OTP, location). Chains are dynamic: a journey can start at any leg and end at any later one. A carton's legs are inherited by every passport inside it. The public report shows every monitored leg: COMPLIANT · NOT COMPLIANT (leg n) · COMPLIANT WHERE MONITORED · JOURNEY IN PROGRESS · NOT YET IN A JOURNEY.
