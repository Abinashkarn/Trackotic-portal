Stage colours — one colour, one word, one number, everywhere

Sidebar tile, queue card, status pill, device page timeline and the printed delivery note all use the same five. Colour never stands alone: the word and the number are always printed with it.

1
RECEIVED · grey
In the building, not tested. No TRK ID yet.
2
QC · amber
On the bench: checklist, firmware. Pass mints the TRK ID.
3
SIM BOUND · READY · blue
SIM fitted, first heartbeat seen. Sellable stock.
4
LABELLED · ALLOCATED · green
QR printed for a tenant = allocated. Then 5 · dispatched, GRN by tenant.
!
FAULT · RETURNED · red
QC fail, field return, recall. Repair → back to 2; scrap → wiped.
