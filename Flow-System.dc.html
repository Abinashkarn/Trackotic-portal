D1
Repeat per leg. Manufacturer HUB → PRO → CFA HUB → PRO → distributor HUB → PRO → retail HUB → PRO → patient. Or just retail HUB → PRO → patient. The system never fixes the count; every bind adds a leg, every handover/next bind closes one. Carton legs are inherited by the units inside.
Passport states: ISSUED → ON_DEVICE ⇄ OFF_DEVICE (never "closed"), VOID only by Track0tic staff with reason. One device at a time: binding to a new device automatically releases the previous binding and closes that leg — no "unbind" step. Receipt-ack is optional, never a gate.
Visibility, fixed: Track0tic staff see every leg with names and places · a tenant sees its own legs plus the one it received from (grants can widen, never narrow) · the patient sees every leg as an anonymous Storage/Transit step with min/max and verdict, the last leg in full; any step with missing readings or no device → COMPLIANT WHERE MONITORED. No names, places or TRK IDs anywhere public. Bank environments (Muthoot, Axis — dedicated) have no public route at all.
